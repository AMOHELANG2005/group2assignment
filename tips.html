Accommodation

Enjoy comfortable lodges, guesthouses, and hotels across Lesotho, ranging from budget to luxury options.

Transport

A 4x4 vehicle is highly recommended for mountain areas due to rough and steep terrain.

Safety

Always travel with a guide in remote areas, keep valuables secure, and stay updated on weather conditions.

Best Time to Travel

The best time to visit is from November to March for warm weather, or June to August for snow experiences.

Local Culture

Respect Basotho traditions, greet locals politely, and experience cultural attire like the Basotho blanket.
