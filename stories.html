Snow in Africa? Yes, it exists.

AfriSki Mountain Resort is located in the Maluti Mountains of Lesotho. It is the only ski resort in Southern Africa, offering a rare experience of snow activities in Africa.

Visitors can enjoy skiing, snowboarding, and breathtaking mountain views. During summer, the resort transforms into an adventure destination with hiking and biking trails.

This makes AfriSki one of the most unique tourism attractions in the region.
