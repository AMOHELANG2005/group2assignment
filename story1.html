Snow in Africa? Yes, it exists.

The idea of snow in Africa often surprises many people, as the continent is widely associated with warm climates and savannah landscapes. However, in the high-altitude regions of Southern Africa, particularly in the Kingdom of Lesotho, winter conditions allow for snowfall and winter sports. One of the most remarkable examples is Afriski Mountain Resort, located in the Maluti Mountains. Situated at an altitude of over 3,000 metres above sea level, Afriski is among the highest ski resorts in Africa and one of the very few in Sub-Saharan Africa (Afriski, 2023; Lesotho Info, 2024).

Afriski Mountain Resort is the only operational ski resort in Lesotho and one of the only ski destinations in Southern Africa. The resort operates mainly during the winter season, from June to August, when temperatures are low enough to support snow-based activities. Due to inconsistent natural snowfall, the resort uses artificial snowmaking technology to ensure reliable skiing conditions, making it accessible to tourists throughout the season (Freeride, 2026; SouthAfrica.net, 2024).

Visitors to Afriski can enjoy a variety of winter activities, including skiing, snowboarding, and snow tubing, all set against the scenic backdrop of the Maluti Mountains. The resort caters to all skill levels, offering training for beginners as well as more advanced slopes for experienced visitors. In addition, Afriski hosts seasonal events and competitions, attracting both local and international tourists, which strengthens its role as a key adventure tourism destination in the region (Lesotho Info, 2024; SouthAfrica.net, 2024).

Beyond winter, Afriski transforms into a year-round tourism destination. During the summer months, the resort offers activities such as hiking, mountain biking, and trail running. This seasonal diversification supports sustainable tourism by maintaining visitor numbers throughout the year and reducing dependence on winter-only tourism (Afriski, 2023).

Ultimately, Afriski Mountain Resort represents a unique blend of geography, climate, and tourism innovation. It challenges common perceptions about Africa’s climate and highlights the environmental diversity of the continent. As a result, Afriski stands out as one of the most distinctive tourism attractions in Southern Africa, offering visitors a rare opportunity to experience snow in Africa.
